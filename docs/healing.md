# Healing tools

The healing family shares one legal envelope: every tool is user-directed or footprint-shape-directed, and every blend is the classic healing math of the EXPIRED US 6587592 (Georgiev, expired 2021-11-19), whose express teaching is cleared prior art. None of these tools may gain PatchMatch-style search (offset propagation or random perturbation), reshuffling, gradient-domain compositing of source gradients, or live per-move classification while the gates recorded in [legal-constraints.md](legal-constraints.md) are active (US 8285055 / US 8340463 / US 8355592 into 2031, US 9058699 to 2029, US 8050498 to Nov 3, 2029); content-driven source selection is allowed only as the exhaustive exemplar search that file clears ("Content-aware fill and exemplar inpainting"). Constraint comments live at the implementation sites (`core/heal_membrane.cpp`, `core/spot_heal.cpp`, `canvas_widget_spot_healing.cpp`, `canvas_widget_patch_tool.cpp`, `canvas_widget_shared.cpp` healing_sample) and must stay in sync with [patent-research.md](patent-research.md).

Two blend engines exist:

- **The healing membrane** (`core/heal_membrane.{hpp,cpp}`, `solve_heal_membrane`): destination-minus-source offsets on the ring of cells just outside the region are interpolated harmonically across the interior (relaxation on the OFFSET field with Dirichlet boundary conditions - the expired patent's teaching, NOT Poisson compositing of gradients), and the healed pixel is source texture plus the smooth offset. The solver is integer 16.16 fixed-point SOR on a coarse-to-fine cascade with fixed sweep counts and lexicographic order, so it is byte-deterministic across toolchains. Spot Healing and the Patch tool's Source/Destination modes use it; it needs no radius knob.
- **The ring tone match** (`canvas_widget_shared.cpp`: `healing_ring_tone`, `healing_sample`): the original per-dab frequency separation, used live per stroke segment by the Healing Brush (its Diffusion 1-7 option maps to the ring radius). The Patch tool's Transparent option instead extracts detail with a real separable box-blur local mean.

## Sample All Layers

Clone, Healing, Spot Healing, and Patch share one Sample All Layers checkbox (canvas-owned `retouch_sample_all_layers_`, key `tools/retouchSampleAllLayers`, default on). Checked keeps the historical behavior: the per-gesture source snapshot is the merged document (`CanvasWidget::retouch_source_snapshot()` wraps `render_document_image_with_processing()`). Unchecked samples the active pixel layer alone through the promoted `active_layer_sample_image` helper (layer mask and opacity folded into alpha), which the Magic Wand and Quick Select sample options also use. The snapshot is taken once at gesture start, never per move. `ui_retouch_sample_all_layers_switches_clone_and_healing_source` pins both states.

## Classic Healing Brush

The Healing Brush is an explicit-source pixel tool: Alt-click picks a source from the gesture snapshot, Aligned shares Clone's across-stroke offset, and Diffusion 1-7 sets the ring radius separating source detail from local tone. Each output sample adds the source center's difference from its alpha-weighted eight-sample source ring to the matching destination ring tone. The stroke shares Clone's size, opacity, softness, selection, transparent-pixel lock, palette snap, undo, and source-snapshot behavior. Healing writes only ordinary layer pixels; PSD/PSB round-trips need no private metadata. `ui_healing_brush_transfers_detail_and_preserves_destination_tone` pins the math and the PSD round trip.

## Spot Healing

Spot Healing removes a blemish with no source pick: the drag accumulates a soft procedural footprint (doc-sized Grayscale8, stamped as brush_coverage capsules, shown as a translucent trail like Quick Select's) and the heal runs once on release in `finish_spot_heal_stroke()`. Escape cancels the footprint before anything is written; `begin_edit` is deferred to release, so a canceled stroke leaves no history entry (a press-time `can_begin_pixel_edit` precheck still reports refusals immediately).

The texture comes from ONE coherent rigid mapping chosen in `core/spot_heal.cpp` (`spot_heal_source_map`): the footprint reflected across the line just past its nearest boundary, or translated clear of its own extent when the reflection would land back inside. Candidate directions (nearest-boundary, opposite, both perpendiculars; reflection before translation) are tried in a fixed order and scored only by mask-geometry validity - pixel content is never read, so there is no patch search. A coherent map keeps the sampled texture spatially continuous (the earlier per-pixel mirror field smeared radial streaks). The membrane then bends the pasted texture's tone into everything around the stroke. Off-canvas never acts as a source; edge blemishes sample from the interior side.

Options: Size, Soft (the footprint skirt feathers the heal), Sample All Layers. There is deliberately no Opacity, no Aligned, no Diffusion (the membrane replaces the ring radius), and no coverage cap: one write per pixel per stroke, outside the brush stroke compositor. Shift-click stroke connect and `[`/`]` sizing work as with the other brushes. Coverage: `spot_heal_source_map_*` and `heal_membrane_interpolates_boundary_offsets` in tests/core/infra_selection_tests.cpp; behavior plus the visual gallery (`ui_spot_healing_*`, `ui_spot_healing_and_patch_texture_gallery`) in tests/ui/brush_engine_stroke_tests_healing_patch.cpp.

## Remove Object

Edit > Remove Object (also in the Select menu; hotkey id `edit.remove_object`, unbound by default; object name `editRemoveObjectAction`) fills the current selection from its surroundings with `CanvasWidget::remove_object_in_selection(method)`. The selection is the footprint and its own feather is the write's skirt; the selection stays put; no dialog. Two methods, of which only the first has a menu entry (the Nearest Edge action was removed from the Edit, Select, and canvas context menus on 2026-09-23 because it confused users next to the content-aware fill; the method stays reachable as the automatic fallback and through the script API):

- **Content-aware (default)**: the exhaustive exemplar fill of `core/exemplar_inpaint.{hpp,cpp}` (Criminisi 2003, whose Microsoft patent expired; the boundary in [legal-constraints.md](legal-constraints.md) "Content-aware fill and exemplar inpainting" and the claim record in [patent-research-inpainting.md](patent-research-inpainting.md)). A copy of the retouch snapshot is filled from the hole boundary inward in priority order (confidence times the strongest known isophote projected on the front normal, integer math; ties go to the cell nearest the original hole edge by a BFS distance transform, then the smaller cell index, so equal priorities peel in from every edge instead of sweeping from the top-left and colliding with the far edge in a seam; priorities are cached per front cell and refreshed only in the neighbourhood of each copied patch, Criminisi's incremental update); each 9x9 target patch takes the best source patch from an exhaustive scan of a window around it, scored by an integer L1 distance over the target's known pixels (SSE2/NEON and scalar paths give the same sums) plus a fixed penalty of 2 per pixel of Manhattan offset between source and target centres (a geometric preference for nearby sources: rows draw from their own row to the left and right, so a grain's phase stays locked to the surroundings instead of drifting until it collides with the far edge, which printed a vertical seam a patch inside the right edge of wide selections), first scan index on ties, with strips sharing the best distance only to prune strictly worse partial sums. The candidates come from up to five full-resolution windows of `radius` = `clamp(max(w, h) / 2 + 32, 48, 96)`: the target's own window, plus, when that window holds fewer than 4096 clean source centres (an integral-image count; the target is deep inside), for each axis ray from the target whose first original known cell lies beyond that window, the half-window on the far side of that edge point (per-cell ray tables), so a target deep in a large hole still sees the band to its left and right at its own height as well as above and below (a coarse candidate grid was tried first and shifted the grain's phase by a pixel or two deep inside, printing a seam where the zones met; a window centred only on the nearest edge pulled the darker wood from below into the middle); a per-row next-valid-centre table skips the hole in O(1), and a last resort scans the whole window on a stride of 4. Filled pixels never become sources, and each search is independent of every other patch's offset (no offset field, propagation, perturbation, pruning, or coarse-to-fine map, by legal design). The candidate scan fans out over row strips with a (distance, index) reduction, so it is byte-identical single-threaded. After the fill, `exemplar_match_tone` runs frequency separation through the cleared membrane: the fill's low-pass band (box blur, radius 24) is replaced by the harmonic interpolation across the hole of the surroundings' low-pass (a normalized blur over original known pixels only, so the removed object never leaks in), keeping the fill's grain; that removes the brightness polygon where fill fronts from differently lit edges meet (no gradients are composited, so US 9058699 stays untouched). The result is written through the shared `heal_mask_from_surroundings` with an identity source map, whose membrane solves to zero. The overlay reads "Removing object... N%" (progress is per patch, throttled to 80 ms). Status: "content-aware fill (N patches)". When no fully known source patch is in reach (the selection fills its neighbourhood), it falls back to Nearest Edge and says so.
- **Nearest Edge** (no QAction or hotkey id; `remove_object_in_selection(RemoveObjectMethod::NearestEdge)` and `layer.removeObject({method: "nearestEdge"})` only): the selection form of Spot Healing, `spot_heal_source_map` plus the membrane through the same write (the stroke path calls the same routine, byte-identical to before the split; areas at or above 262,144 pixels fan out over row strips like the Patch tool). Instant, and the status bar reports "source N of M".

Both refuse (status error, no history entry) without a selection, without unselected pixels around the selection to sample, or when `can_begin_pixel_edit` refuses.

**Try another (Nearest Edge).** Running it again on the same selection (same bounds and an FNV-1a hash of its coverage) advances `remove_object_attempt_`; `spot_heal_source_map`'s `attempt` parameter indexes the geometrically valid candidates in their fixed order (mirror then translate per direction: nearest boundary, opposite, the two perpendiculars) and wraps, so attempt 0 is the historical pick and Spot Healing itself is unchanged. The user's repeat count is the only input besides shape; pixel content is never read in that mode. Undo does not reset the cycle, so Undo / Remove Object / Undo / Remove Object also walks the candidates; a different selection restarts at 0. Content-aware runs do not touch the cycle.

Three more entry points share the action: the canvas context menu (a right-click on the selection offers Remove Object; see "Canvas right-click menu" in [tools.md](tools.md)), the Patch tool (Enter with an outline and no drag runs the content-aware fill, Enter mid-drag commits the drag like a release, and the `patchRemoveObjectButton` options-bar button triggers the same action), and the script API `layer.removeObject({method?, attempt?})`, which returns `{method, patches, source, sourceCount}`, requires the layer to be the active layer, and rides the run's single undo snapshot (`record_history = false` runs the prechecks without `begin_edit`). Coverage: `exemplar_inpaint_restores_periodic_stripes_deterministically` and `spot_heal_source_map_attempt_cycles_valid_candidates` (core), `ui_remove_object_*` (including `ui_remove_object_parallel_heal_detaches_shared_pixels` for the strip path with shared bytes), `ui_patch_tool_enter_removes_object`, the Remove Object rows of `ui_spot_healing_and_patch_texture_gallery`, `ui_selection_context_menu_offers_remove_object` (tests/ui/layer_context_lifecycle_tests.cpp), and `ui_script_remove_object_heals_selection` plus the local-fixture `ui_script_remove_object_then_reopen_large_document` (tests/ui/scripting_tests.cpp).

## Patch tool

The Patch tool is a selection tool plus a heal: drawing reuses the lasso machinery wholesale (freehand outline, New/Add/Subtract/Intersect combine modes, feather and anti-alias, its own slot in the per-tool combine-mode table), or an existing selection of any origin can be used. A press inside the selection in Replace mode starts the drag; Shift/Alt force outline drawing (Photoshop parity). The drag previews a raw translated blit of the frozen gesture snapshot plus a statically dashed copy of the outline; nothing recomposites and nothing is classified per move. Release below the drag threshold is a no-op; Escape, tool switches, edit locks, and document teardown cancel the drag with no history entry; Enter commits a live drag like a release, and Enter with an outline and no drag runs Remove Object (below). Because the two-step workflow is not discoverable, the tool announces it: switching to Patch shows a how-to status line, and committing an outline shows the mode-appropriate "drag the selection..." hint (Source mode adds the Enter shortcut).

The commit in `commit_patch_tool_drag()` runs once on release, with the drag offset as the only source choice. Modes:

- **Source** (default): the dragged-from region takes the dragged-to texture plus the membrane solved over the region (boundary offsets = destination minus source on the ring of uncovered cells, padded one cell). The selection stays put.
- **Destination**: the region is copied onto the drop point through the same membrane with roles swapped, and the selection follows the copy as its own history step after the pixel step.
- **Transparent** (both modes): texture-only transfer - destination pixel plus the source's high-pass detail (source minus its separable box-blurred local mean at the region-derived radius), with the membrane solved on the negated boundary detail so the overlay's edge residual fades to zero instead of printing the selection outline; destination alpha is kept. Photoshop parity note: on same-texture content the option is nearly a no-op by design; it exists for stamping distinct marks over plain backgrounds.

Writes follow the clone conventions: selection coverage from the drag mask, transparent-pixel lock, palette snap, layer expansion, out-of-canvas sources skipped (Dirichlet offsets use clamped snapshot reads so the solve is defined everywhere). Areas at or above 262,144 pixels fan out the blend over row strips (`std::async` plus `max_blocking_fanout_workers`, `PATCHY_RENDER_SINGLE_THREADED` honored); the membrane solves once up front and every healed pixel is a pure function of the snapshot, the mask, the offset, and the solved membrane, so the fan-out is byte-identical to the sequential walk. Both heal writers detach the layer's copy-on-write bytes on the calling thread before fanning out (the undo snapshot shares them; see the AGENTS.md invariant) and the strips write through one span. Mode and Transparent are session-only: every startup begins at Source with Transparent off (a persisted Destination or Transparent reads as "the tool is broken" a session later); mid-session choices still propagate to new documents. The retired keys `tools/patchMode` and `tools/patchTransparent` must never be reused. Coverage: `ui_patch_tool_*`, `ui_patch_options_sync_canvas_and_persist`, and the texture gallery in tests/ui/brush_engine_stroke_tests_healing_patch.cpp.

## Palette placement

The Healing flyout (`healingToolButton`) holds Healing Brush (J, the default action), Spot Healing (Shift+J), and Patch (unbound, like Sponge and Sharpen). Hotkey ids `tools.healing`, `tools.spot_healing`, `tools.patch` are persisted contracts. Icon silhouettes stay distinct at palette size: bandage (Healing), circle with burst ticks (Spot Healing), rotated stitched square (Patch).
